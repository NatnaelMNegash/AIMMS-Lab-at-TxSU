Research

Dynamics, intelligence, and safety—designed as one system.

Principled methods for autonomous ground and aerial systems, from understanding human interaction to planning energy-aware operations.

01

Human-Centered Autonomous Driving

Decision-making and control strategies that help autonomous vehicles anticipate, interpret, and respond safely to human road users.

Methods

Game theory, behavior modeling, motion prediction, and safe control.

Applications

Mixed traffic, lane changing, and automated platoons.

02

Safe Learning & Optimal Control

Architectures combining optimization, learning, and formal safety for uncertain dynamic systems.

Methods

MPPI, MPC, CBFs, reinforcement learning, and state estimation.

Applications

Safety-critical navigation and real-time autonomy.

03

Advanced Air Mobility

Energy-aware planning, dispatching, and charging coordination for electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft.

Methods

Distributed optimization, scheduling, and battery modeling.

Applications

eVTOL operations and vertiport planning.

04

Aerial & Multi-Robot Systems

Intelligent planning and coordination for aerial vehicles and heterogeneous robot teams.

Methods

Path planning, multi-agent coordination, and learning-based control.

Applications

UAV missions, exploration, and emergency response.
