Our mission

Engineering autonomy people can trust.

Led by Natnael M. Negash, Ph.D., the laboratory investigates how dynamic systems can perceive uncertainty, reason about interaction, and act safely. Graduate and undergraduate researchers work together across modeling, simulation, learning, optimization, and experimental validation.

Research portfolio

From human behavior to coordinated mobility.

Our work spans the autonomy stack while remaining anchored in dynamics, control, and measurable safety.

Human-Centered Autonomous Driving
