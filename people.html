Natnael M. Negash, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Ingram School of Engineering · Texas State University

Dr. Negash specializes in dynamics, control, and intelligent systems. His research develops safe, efficient, and socially responsive solutions for autonomous ground vehicles, aerial vehicles, and eVTOL platforms. He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Texas Tech University in 2024, an M.Sc. from Addis Ababa University, and a B.Sc. from Adama Science and Technology University.
